# BUA-Cement-vs-Dangote-Cement-Five-Year-Financial-Analysis-2021-2025-
MA Talent Project 

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive financial performance analysis and comparison of BUA Cement Plc and Dangote Cement Plc over five financial years (2021–2025).

The analysis was conducted using Microsoft Excel to evaluate the companies' profitability, financial health, cash flow performance, earnings trends and overall financial strength.

The objective was to identify which company demonstrates stronger financial performance, consistency and financial sustainability, providing evidence-based insights to support investment evaluation and business decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Compare the financial performance of BUA Cement and Dangote Cement.
- Evaluate revenue growth and profitability trends.
- Assess liquidity and financial leverage.
- Analyse cash flow generation and earnings quality.
- Compare earnings per share (EPS) performance.
- Develop a financial scorecard to evaluate both companies.
- Identify financial risks and opportunities.
- Provide a data-driven recommendation based on the findings.

## Financial Metrics Analysed

The project evaluates seven major financial performance indicators:

1. Revenue Growth: Measures the rate at which each company's revenue increases over time.
2. Net Profit Margin: Evaluates how much profit a company retains from its revenue.
3. Return on Equity (ROE): Measures how effectively shareholders' equity generates profit.
4. Cash Ratio: Assesses the ability to meet short-term obligations using available cash.
5. Debt-to-Equity Ratio: Evaluates financial leverage and dependence on borrowing.
6. Free Cash Flow (FCF): Measures cash remaining after capital expenditure.
7. Earnings Per Share (EPS) Trend: Evaluates the growth of earnings attributable to each share.

Additional analysis includes Price-to-Earnings (P/E) ratios and operating cash flow-to-profit ratios.

## Key Findings and Insights

The analysis produced the following major findings:

1. Revenue Growth

- BUA Cement recorded an average revenue growth of approximately 43.1%.
- Dangote Cement recorded approximately 34.2%.
- BUA demonstrated stronger average revenue growth.

2. Profitability

- Dangote Cement recorded an average net profit margin of approximately 36.1%.
- BUA Cement recorded approximately 23.3%.
- Dangote demonstrated stronger and more consistent profitability.

3. Return on Equity

- Dangote Cement recorded an average ROE of approximately 34.6%.
- BUA Cement recorded approximately 30.4%.

4. Financial Leverage

- Dangote Cement recorded an average debt-to-equity ratio of approximately 0.51x.
- BUA Cement recorded approximately 0.95x.
- Dangote demonstrated lower financial leverage.

5. Free Cash Flow

- Dangote Cement generated approximately ₦2.92 trillion in cumulative free cash flow over five years.
- BUA Cement generated approximately ₦712 billion.
- Dangote demonstrated stronger cash generation.

6. Overall Financial Assessment

Based on the seven financial indicators, Dangote Cement emerged as the preferred company for further consideration, with moderate confidence.

BUA Cement also demonstrated strengths in revenue growth, cash holdings and its 2025 performance.

## Business Recommendation

Based on the five-year financial analysis, Dangote Cement demonstrated stronger overall financial consistency, profitability, cash generation and debt management.

However, BUA Cement remains a competitive company with significant revenue growth and improving profitability.

The recommendation is based on historical business performance and does not establish whether either company's shares are undervalued or overvalued. It is not a guarantee of future investment returns or personal financial advice.

## Data Quality and Limitations

- The analysis covers the 2021–2025 financial years.
- Financial figures are expressed in millions of Nigerian naira (₦ million), except EPS and share prices.
- BUA Cement's 2022 and 2023 figures were cross-checked against the uploaded financial statements.
- Other years and Dangote Cement figures require further verification against published annual reports.
- The original case study mentioned BUA Cement versus HBM (formerly Lafarge Africa), but the available working dataset compared BUA Cement against Dangote Cement. This analysis follows the available dataset.
- Macroeconomic factors, inflation and foreign exchange movements may affect reported financial performance.
